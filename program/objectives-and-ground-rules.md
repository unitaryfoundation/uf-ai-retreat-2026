# Purpose and ground rules

An invite-only retreat for people using AI tools to advance quantum information. Accommodation and meals are all on site. The weekend is organised around discussion and collaboration, not presentations and formal talks.

## Three objectives

1. Swap notes on how best to use AI tools to accelerate research.
2. Coordinate to be more ambitious in the problems we solve together.
3. Discuss structures for doing better research in the new paradigm: agentic leaderboards, Lean repos, zoos, review and credit norms.

## Ground rules

- Limited seated-audience talks and no slides.
- Breaks are long on purpose, so conversations and groupings can evolve naturally.
- Walk-and-talk sessions in small groups use the grounds and campus buildings.

## Pre-work

Each guest filled out a pre-read, and was asked to drop one reusable thing into this repo before arriving: a skill, a prompt, a harness config, an `AGENTS.md`, a verifier script, with a two-line README. See [`toolbox/`](../toolbox/).

The pre-read is used to:

- generate one page per person in [`attendees/`](../attendees/INDEX.md), printed for the Friday gallery;
- pick the five workflow stations from the demo offers;
- build cross-cluster trios for the walk from backgrounds and 10x problems;
- seed the three live charts in [`charts/`](../charts/);
- give Saturday's pitch session its starting inventory.
