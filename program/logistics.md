# Logistics

Organiser contact details, the wifi password and other private details are deliberately not in this repo. They are in the program document emailed to attendees. On site, ask Cody Moy or Veena Vijayakumar.

## Venue and accommodation

Hilltown Commons, 63 Huyck Road, Rensselaerville, NY. Unitary Foundation covers lodging and on-site meals from November 13 to 15. If you arrive early or stay on in the area, those nights are your own to arrange and cover.

## Getting there

- Fly into Albany International Airport (ALB).
- Take Amtrak to Albany-Rensselaer (ALB).
- Drive directly to Hilltown Commons.

From the airport or station, book a rideshare or taxi; Unitary Foundation will reimburse it.

## Check-in

At the Guggenheim Pavilion from 3:00 PM on Friday, November 13. You will receive your keys and be shown to your room. If you expect to arrive after 5:00 PM, tell the organisers as soon as possible so a late check-in can be arranged.

## What to bring

Upstate New York is cold in November and snow is likely. Bring a winter coat, boots, scarf, gloves and a hat; you will walk outdoors between buildings. Most buildings are within a five to ten minute walk of each other.

## Departure

Check out on the morning of Sunday, November 15; leave your keys in your room. To-go lunches are provided. Two shuttles are arranged, each about 45 to 60 minutes:

- 12:00 PM to Albany International Airport
- 12:30 PM to Albany-Rensselaer Amtrak station

There are no rideshare options at the venue, so take a shuttle unless you drove. If you have not requested a seat, ask the organisers.
