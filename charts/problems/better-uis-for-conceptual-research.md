# Better interfaces for AI-assisted conceptual research

Came up at [Andi Gu's workflow station](../../sessions/sat-workflow-stations/andi-gu.md) on Saturday morning. Will Zeng relayed it and Claude wrote it up.

## Problem

Good harnesses and interfaces exist for research that has a validated, quantifiable objective function: agents iterate against a score you trust. Much research is conceptual instead, such as finding the right definitions, framing, or question to ask. There, the available interfaces are mostly very unsatisfying. What would a good interface for AI-assisted conceptual research look like, as opposed to numerical work or work on code?

## Verifier

Not settled. One candidate: researchers doing conceptual work choose the new interface over chat and a terminal, and can point to an idea or reframing that came out of using it. Sharpening this is part of the problem.

## Why 10x throughput changes it

Agent throughput is easy to spend when there is a score to optimise. Without an objective, the bottleneck is the human's ability to steer, read and judge many lines of thought at once, which is an interface problem. More throughput only helps conceptual work if the interface can absorb it.
