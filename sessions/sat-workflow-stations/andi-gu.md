## What breaks

The discussion split AI-assisted research into two kinds:

1. **Work with a validated, quantifiable objective function.** You have a number to push up or down, and a check you trust. Good harnesses and interfaces already exist for this, and agents can iterate against the score.
2. **Conceptual work.** Finding the right definitions or framing, deciding which question to ask, building understanding. There is no scalar objective here, and current interfaces are mostly very unsatisfying for it.
